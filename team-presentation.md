## ❓ **Anticipated Questions & Answers**

### **"How much maintenance will this actually require?"**

**Initial Setup**: 4-6 hours total team time  
**Ongoing**: 1-2 hours per week maximum  
**Most content is evergreen** - won't need frequent updates  
**Updates happen naturally** as team learns new information  

### **"What if users don't actually use it?"**

**Proactive approach**:
- Pin prominently in all Discord channels
- Team members link to specific sections when answering
- Much faster than typing explanations repeatedly
- Mobile-friendly for users on phones

### **"How do we ensure accuracy and keep it updated?"**

**Quality control process**:
- Assign rotating update responsibilities
- Monthly accuracy review with team
- User feedback integration
- Version control through GitHub

### **"Why GitHub instead of Notion/other platforms?"**

**GitHub advantages**:
- Free and accessible to everyone
- Version control and change tracking
- Professional appearance
- Mobile-friendly
- Easy linking from Discord
- No account required for viewing
