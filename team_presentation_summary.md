# 📊 Chutes AI Knowledge Base - Team Presentation

## 🎯 **Executive Summary**

### **The Problem**
Our Discord support channel is overwhelmed with repetitive questions. Analysis of 277+ conversations reveals:
- **35% billing/subscription issues** (mostly can't cancel)
- **25% model configuration confusion** (conflicting parameter info)  
- **20% performance problems** (models going "cold")
- **Scammers targeting users** with fake support

### **The Solution**
Comprehensive knowledge base addressing all major pain points with:
- ✅ Immediate fixes for critical issues
- ✅ Clear escalation paths  
- ✅ Scam protection guidance
- ✅ Professional team reference

### **Expected Impact**
- 📉 **60-80% reduction** in repetitive support questions
- ⚡ **Faster resolution** times for users
- 🛡️ **Better security** awareness
- 👥 **Improved team efficiency**
